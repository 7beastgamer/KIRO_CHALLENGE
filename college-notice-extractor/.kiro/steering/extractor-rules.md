# College Announcement Extraction Guidelines

When processing files in `notices/raw/`:

1. Target Output: Standardized Markdown checklist saved to `notices/extracted/`.

2. Extraction Fields Required:

   - What Changed: Concise 1–2 sentence summary of policy, schedule, or facility changes.

   - Target Audience: Specific student groups affected (e.g., Seniors, Resident Students, Financial Aid Applicants).

   - Important Dates & Deadlines: Bulleted chronological list of hard dates.

   - Action Checklist: Checkbox list (- [ ]) formatted with mandatory student steps.

3. Tone & Style: Direct, actionable, clear, and devoid of administrative fluff.
