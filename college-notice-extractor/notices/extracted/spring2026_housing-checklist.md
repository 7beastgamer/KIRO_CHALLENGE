# Spring 2026 Housing Re-application — Action Checklist

## What Changed

All undergraduate residence hall students must now submit a room renewal request for Spring 2026 or risk losing priority housing status. A $50 deposit is required at the time of submission.

- **Undergraduate students** currently living in university residence halls
- **Graduating seniors** (additional key hand-in requirement)

---

- **October 15, 2026 at 11:59 PM** — Room renewal request submission deadline (loss of priority housing if missed)
- **May 10, 2026** — Physical room key hand-in for graduating seniors
